## Preconditions
The harness has registered the four fixture cameras and waited for them to stream. Search results include person crops.

## Goal
Use a person crop to find and compare visually similar appearances.

## Expected Outcomes
- [E1] Starting "Find similar" from an appearance opens a cosine-ranked list whose first results include crops from the same camera as the source.
- [E2] The source appearance remains identifiable by its crop, camera name, and time.
