## Preconditions
The harness has registered the four fixture cameras and waited for them to stream. Person crops are available before searching.

## Goal
Find a person by an English description and narrow the results to one camera.

## Expected Outcomes
- [E1] Searching for "a person walking" returns at least one result with a person crop, camera name, and time.
- [E2] Filtering to one camera narrows the results to that camera.
