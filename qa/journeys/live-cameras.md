## Preconditions
The harness has registered the four fixture cameras and waited for them to stream.

## Goal
Confirm that the configured camera sources can be monitored on the live wall and that person detection is visible.

## Expected Outcomes
- [E1] The Cameras page lists all four fixture cameras.
- [E2] The authenticated live wall shows advancing video from at least one fixture camera within 30 seconds.
- [E3] A detection overlay or person indicator appears for at least one fixture camera within 2 minutes.
